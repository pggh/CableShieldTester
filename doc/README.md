# Measurement examples

**Important: play all videos with sound - the measurement result is acoustical.**

## An unshielded audio cable

The ground pin is already connected. When the signal is applied to one of the audio lines, it is coupled to the sense line.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/809c1926-0d2f-4c97-92ae-36a32c1142be

## A shielded audio cable

The ground pin is already connected. When the signal is applied to one of the audio lines, nothing happens because the signal is shielded.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/3892af2c-89ef-467d-8cf5-392d25709fa2

## Common shield for both channels

The ground pin as well as the signal pin are already connected.
When connecting the sense input to the other audio channel, the signal is heavily coupled to it.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/de1b4ab3-1894-417e-95cb-727351e04f2a

## Individual shield for both channels

The ground pin as well as the signal pin are already connected.
When connecting the sense input to the other audio channel, the signal is slightly coupled to it.
Ideally no signal should leak through. The slight coupling could be within the connector or the shield is not that great.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/d68851f2-6222-4acf-88ee-22f93a323d15

## Unshielded USB cable

The signal pin is already connected to one of the USB signals. It is coupled to the sense line.
The coupling is not stopped, when connecting ground line to the shell of the connector.

https://github.com/user-attachments/assets/f3afa772-5d94-4523-8f89-95132691fe7d

## Shielded USB cable

The signal pin is already connected to one of the USB signals. It is coupled to the sense line.
When connecting the ground line to the shell of the connector, the signal coupling stops.
